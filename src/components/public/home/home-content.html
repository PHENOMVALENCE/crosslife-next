About

CrossLife Mission Network

CrossLife Mission Network (CMN) is a non-denominational and inter-denominational Christian ministry based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We exist as a community of Life, Love, Sonship, and Prayer, welcoming people from diverse backgrounds, ages, and walks of life.

Our Mandate

To manifest Sons of God who know who they are in Christ, and what Christ can do through them.

Our Vision

To be a global network of the manifested Sons of God walking in the Life of God, expressing the New Creation realities through experiential knowledge, and advancing the Eternal Ordinations of Christ across the nations with one message, one mandate, and one mission.

Our Mission

To reach the global network by revealing Christ as the Way, the Truth, and the Life; equipping Sons of God to serve effectively in the Kingdom of God through discipleship training, revelational teaching of the (Gospel (s) of the Cross, Glorification, Kingdom of God, and Immortality), spiritual relevancy formation, and life-filled koinonia—manifesting His Spirit, Love, Life, and Power in the Church and the marketplace.

Our Convictions

Statement of Faith | Our Convictions | Our Beliefs

CrossLife Mission Network (CMN) is founded on the following doctrinal convictions that uphold the Gospel of the Cross (Love &Grace), Sonship, Kingdom (influence in the Marketplace) and Immortality.

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible and authoritative Word of God — the final standard for faith and life. It is living, active, and sharper than any two-edged sword.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 • Hebrews 4:12

The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in nature, power, and glory, and worthy of all worship.

Deuteronomy 6:4 • Matthew 28:19

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ — His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death on the Cross, bodily resurrection, and His imminent return in glory.

John 1:1–4 • Isaiah 7:14

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit indwells every believer, empowering them for service, guiding into all truth, and manifesting the character and gifts of Christ.

John 14:16–17 • Acts 1:8

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body and Bride of Christ — called to advance His Kingdom on earth through worship, discipleship, fellowship, and mission.

Ephesians 1:22–23 • Matthew 28:18–20

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is a free gift of God, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, but by the finished work of the Cross.

Ephesians 2:8–9 • Romans 3:24

New Creation Realities

We believe that in Christ, the believer becomes a new creation — born of God, seated with Christ, and empowered to live in sonship and divine identity.

2 Corinthians 5:17 • John 1:12–13

Kingdom Influence

We believe the Kingdom of God is both present and coming — and that believers are called to manifest Kingdom realities and influence every sphere of society.

Matthew 6:10 • Romans 8:19

Gospel in the Marketplace

We believe God has called His people to carry the dominion mandate — being salt and light in every sector: business, government, education, arts, and beyond.

Genesis 1:26–28 • Matthew 5:13–16

Meet Our Senior Pastor

Pastor Lenhard Kyamba

Apostolic Head

Pastor Lenhard Kyamba (PLK) is a Minister of Life leading an army of Sons of God through CrossLife Mission Network, the School of Christ, and the Kingdom Business Forum—with a mandate to manifest Sons of God who know their identity in Christ. He is a practicing Advocate (Corporate, International Commerce & Maritime Law), holds a Master's in International Trade and Maritime Law and a BA in Theology and Leadership, and is author of Awakened Person, Manifesting Sons of God, Kingdom Profile, Man Awake, Woman Awake, among other Kingdom resources.
